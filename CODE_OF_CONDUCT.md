# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want TypeSafe-as-a-Judge to be a welcoming, useful, and respectful project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical decisions.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and focus on the work.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and first-time contributors.
- Protect private information and never post credentials or sensitive customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private data, or security-sensitive details.
- Disruptive behavior intended to prevent others from participating.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile: [github.com/E-FL](https://github.com/E-FL). Do not turn a conduct concern into a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
